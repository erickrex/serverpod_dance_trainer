## 4. How To Enter

Entrants may enter by visiting [the Hackathon Website](https://builderbase.dev/event/build-something-real-the-serverpod-hackathon) and following the steps below:

- Register for the Hackathon on the Hackathon Website. To complete registration, create a free BuilderBase account or log in with an existing BuilderBase account. This enables you to receive important updates and to create your Submission.
- Complete the required registration questions, which include: (i) what best describes your current role; and (ii) what you currently use for your app backends.
- Obtain access to the required developer tools and platform and complete a Project as described under Project Requirements below. Use of the developer tools is subject to the licence agreement relating to them.
- Complete and enter all of the required fields on the submission page of the Hackathon Website (each a "Submission") during the Submission Period, following the Submission Requirements below.
- To be considered for the Most Valuable Feedback prize, Entrants must be registered for the Hackathon on the Hackathon Website, submit an eligible Submission, and complete the feedback form (each a "Feedback Submission") during the Feedback Period. To be eligible, a Feedback Submission must be complete and contain actionable comments that Serverpod can use to improve the Serverpod SDKs, App Studio or related documentation, for example bug reports, user interface improvements, or suggested integrations. One Feedback Submission per Entrant.

Entry in the Hackathon constitutes acknowledgement that the Sponsor and the Administrator will process an Entrant's personal data for the purpose of operating, judging and publicising the Hackathon, as described in section 15.

### Project Requirements

**What to Create:** Entrants must build a full-stack application in which Serverpod is the backend (each a "Project"). There are no tracks. The Project may be practical, playful or experimental, but it must be a working full-stack app built on the Serverpod stack.

**Functionality:** The Project must be capable of being successfully installed and run consistently on the platform for which it is intended, and must function as depicted in the video and described in the text description.

**Platforms:** A submitted Project must run on the platform for which it is intended and which is specified in the Submission Requirements.

**New Projects Only:** Projects must be newly created by the Entrant during the Submission Period.

**Third Party Integrations:** If a Project integrates any third-party SDK, API or data, the Entrant must be authorized to use it in accordance with the applicable terms and licensing requirements.

**AI and agentic tools:** Use of AI coding assistants and agentic tooling is expressly permitted and encouraged. Entrants must comply with the licence terms of any AI tool, model or component incorporated in a Project, and **must disclose their use in the text description**.

### Submission Requirements

Submissions must meet the following requirements:

- Include a Project built with the required developer tools that meets the Project Requirements above.
- Provide a URL to a code repository for judging and testing. The repository must contain all source code, assets and instructions required for the Project to be functional. Where the Hackathon Website creates a private repository under Serverpod's GitHub organisation on the Entrant's behalf, that repository satisfies this requirement. Where an Entrant supplies its own private repository, the Entrant must share it with viktor@serverpod.dev, alexander@serverpod.dev and isak@serverpod.dev.
- Include a text description explaining the features and functionality of the Project and how it was built.
- Include instructions for how to build and run the application.
- Include a demonstration video of the Project. The video:
  - should be less than two (2) minutes, and Judges are not required to watch beyond two minutes;
  - should show the Project functioning on the device for which it was built;
  - must be uploaded to and made publicly visible on YouTube or Vimeo, with a link provided on the submission form; and
  - **must not include third-party trade marks, or copyrighted music or other material, unless the Entrant has permission to use it.**

The demonstration video and the text description are mandatory and are the primary material the Judges rely on. Presentation quality is not scored separately under the Judging Criteria in section 6, so Entrants should spend their time on the Project rather than on video production.

### Multiple Submissions

An Entrant may submit more than one Submission, however each Submission must be unique and substantially different from each of that Entrant's other Submissions, as determined by the Sponsor and the Administrator in their sole discretion.

### Testing

Access must be provided to a working Project for judging and testing, by providing a link to a website, a functioning demo, or a test build. If the Entrant's website is private, the Entrant must include login credentials in its testing instructions. The Entrant must make the Project available free of charge and without restriction for testing, evaluation and use by the Sponsor, the Administrator and the Judges until the Judging Period ends. Judges are not required to test the Project and may judge on the basis of the text description, images and video alone.

### Language Requirements

All Submission materials must be in English or, if not in English, the Entrant must provide an English translation of the demonstration video, text description, testing instructions and all other materials submitted.

### Team Representation

If a Team or Organization is entering, they must appoint and authorize one Representative to represent, act and enter a Submission on their behalf. The Representative must meet the eligibility requirements above. By entering a Submission on behalf of a Team or Organization you represent and warrant that you are the Representative authorized to act on its behalf.

## 6. Judges and Criteria

Eligible Submissions will be evaluated by a panel of judges selected by the Sponsor (the "Judges"). Judges may be employees of the Sponsor or third parties, may or may not be listed individually on the Hackathon Website, and may change before or during the Judging Period. Judging may take place in one or more rounds with one or more panels of Judges, at the discretion of the Sponsor.

### Stage One: eligibility screening

The first stage determines, on a pass or fail basis, whether a Submission meets a baseline level of viability, namely that the Project reasonably fits the theme and makes reasonable use of the Serverpod stack.

**Automated assistance.** The Administrator uses automated, AI-assisted tooling in Stage One to check that a Submission is complete, to verify that the required elements are present, and to produce a written summary of the Project for the Judges. Every Submission that is not clearly incomplete is reviewed by a person before it is failed at Stage One. Automated tooling does not score Submissions and does not select winners. All Prize decisions are made by the human Judges.

### Stage Two: scored judging

All Submissions that pass Stage One are scored by the Judges against the following weighted criteria (the "Judging Criteria"):

| Criterion | Weight | What the Judges are looking for |
|---|---|---|
| Does it work | 30% | It runs, the core flow completes, nothing critical is faked |
| Use of the Serverpod stack | 25% | Doing real work, not sitting behind a static page |
| Craft and technical creativity | 25% | Rough is fine, careless is not |
| Usefulness | 20% | A clear user with a clear problem, and this helps |

The scores from the Judges determine the potential winners of the applicable Prizes. The Entrants eligible for a Prize whose Submissions earn the highest overall scores under the Judging Criteria become the potential winners of that Prize.

### Best Hackathon Post

The Best Hackathon Post Prize is selected by the Sponsor, at its sole discretion, from public posts made about the Hackathon or about an Entrant's Project. It is not decided by a vote and is not scored under the Judging Criteria.

To be eligible, a post must be published publicly during the Best Hackathon Post Period by an Entrant who is registered for the Hackathon, must clearly identify the Hackathon, and must comply with the Code of Conduct in section 16 and with the terms of the platform on which it is published. The Sponsor looks for posts that show real work in progress or a finished Project and that are genuinely useful or interesting to other developers. An Entrant may publish more than one post, and only the Entrant's best post is considered.

The Sponsor may decline to award this Prize if, in its judgement, no post merits it. The Sponsor's decision is final.

### Tie breaking

If two or more Submissions are tied for a Prize, the tied Submission with the higher score on "Does it work" is treated as the higher scoring Submission. If a tie remains, the process is repeated against each remaining Judging Criterion in the order listed above. If two or more Submissions remain tied on all criteria, the panel of Judges will vote on the tied Submissions. No tie is resolved by chance.

## 16. Code of Conduct

All Entrants, Judges, mentors and other participants must comply with the Hackathon Code of Conduct, which applies to the Hackathon Website, the Hackathon Discord server, and all other Hackathon-related channels and communications.

The following behaviour is not tolerated: harassment, intimidation, bullying or discrimination based on age, disability, gender reassignment, sex, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, marital or civil partnership status, pregnancy or maternity, race, colour, ethnic or national origin, nationality, religion or belief, or any other characteristic protected under applicable law; unwelcome sexual attention, sexual comments or imagery, and any other form of sexual harassment; verbal or written abuse, threats, or violent language; deliberate intimidation or stalking, including online; sustained disruption of judging, presentations or community channels; harassment of Judges, mentors or organisers, including attempts to influence judging outside the process set out in these Official Rules; and retaliation against any person who reports a concern.

Reports may be made to nate@builderbase.com. All reports will be treated seriously and handled with discretion, and information will be shared only with those who need to know in order to address the concern or to comply with a legal obligation.

The Sponsor and the Administrator may take any action they reasonably consider appropriate in response to a breach, including a warning, removal from the Hackathon Website and community channels, disqualification from the Hackathon, withdrawal of eligibility for any Prize, and referral to the relevant authorities.

For questions about these Official Rules, send an email to nate@builderbase.com.
